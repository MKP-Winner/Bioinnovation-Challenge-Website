From APOE ε4 to other genes.

The “AMO-4” name you mentioned is likely a reference to APOE ε4, a well-known Alzheimer’s risk-associated allele. This page separates risk-associated genes from rare deterministic genes so the interface does not overstate what genetic data can tell us.

Science note: APOE ε4 increases risk but does not mean an individual will develop Alzheimer’s. Rare variants in APP, PSEN1, and PSEN2 are associated with dominantly inherited Alzheimer’s disease.

How to structure the project

Separate “signal” from “diagnosis.”

Your eventual database can store sequence or variant information, research metadata, and study outcomes while your interface clearly labels what is known versus uncertain.

Risk-associated

Examples include APOE ε4 and certain TREM2 variants. These are studied as factors that can change probability or biological pathways—not as a binary diagnosis.

Rare disease-causing variants

Examples include specific variants in APP, PSEN1, and PSEN2. These are linked to rare dominantly inherited Alzheimer’s disease.
